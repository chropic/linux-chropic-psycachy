Installed guest observations (exact ISO hash recorded in run-metadata.txt):

- Detached first boot reached GDM, then the QA account reached the Ubuntu 26.10 first-run welcome screen.
- `uname -r`: `7.3.0-10-generic` (generic fallback; the pinned `7.3.1-psycachy` candidate did not boot).
- `mokutil --sb-state`: `SecureBoot enabled`.
- `/sys/kernel/security/lockdown`: `none [integrity] confidentiality`.
- `/sys/module/module/parameters/sig_enforce`: `Y`.
- `dpkg --audit`: no output.
- `sudo apt-get check`: completed package-list, dependency-tree, and state-information checks with no reported error.
- `dkms status`: `nvidia/615.71.09` installed for `7.3.0-10-generic`, `7.3.0-9-generic`, and `7.3.1-psycachy`.
- `modinfo -k 7.3.1-psycachy --field signer nvidia`: `PsyCachy DKMS Module Signing`.
- `sbverify --list /boot/vmlinuz-7.3.1-psycachy`: signature subject and issuer `/CN=PsyCachy Secure Boot Kernel`.
- `mokutil --test-key` reported both `kernel.der` and `module.der` are not enrolled. The installed read-only `psycachy-secureboot.sh check` therefore failed closed at the kernel key check. No MOK enrollment or firmware trust change was made.
- Installer log ended with: `PsyCachy packages installed. Ubuntu remains the default pending activation.` It also recorded a warning that `/etc/apt/` could not be read during a package step; the post-boot `apt-get check` completed successfully and `dpkg --audit` was empty.
- The QEMU virtio-GPU guest has no RTX 4070/NVIDIA device; NVIDIA module load, graphics acceleration, and hardware attestation were not qualified.

Observed live-session reboot anomaly: after installer completion, `sudo -n reboot` from the Try/Rescue live session displayed `KERNEL PANIC ... Attempted to kill init! exitcode=0x0000000b`. The installed disk subsequently booted to GDM after explicitly selecting the hard disk. This was a live-session reboot path, not the detached installed-system boot.
